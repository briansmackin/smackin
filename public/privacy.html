Prepared November 11, 2026. Proposed notice for the QuickBooks connector operated by Smackin', Inc. This draft must be checked against the deployed ERP and its approved service providers before it becomes effective.

Information involved

The planned connection can access company identity, chart of accounts, customer and vendor records, invoices, bills, payments, journal entries, sales records and financial reports. Such records may contain names, business contact information, balances and transaction details. Intuit provides authorization tokens after an authorized company administrator consents.

Purpose and access

The intended purpose is SMACKIN's internal accounting reconciliation, management reporting and operational analysis. Access should be limited to authorized company personnel and approved service providers with a business need.

Connector behavior

The supplied connector implementation encrypts its saved Intuit tokens using AES-256-GCM, keeps the encryption key outside the token file, and retrieves data only through an approved set of read endpoints. It does not persist report or transaction payloads. Deployment settings, backups, proxy logs and downstream ERP handling require separate verification.

ERP and AI processing

The intended recipient is SMACKIN ERP, including its authorized Grok Bot workflow. Before enabling this connection, SMACKIN must confirm which hosting, AI and other service providers receive data, their access restrictions and retention arrangements. This draft makes no assurance about model training or provider retention that has not been verified.

Retention and disconnection

Connector tokens are retained while the connection is authorized. A successful disconnect request revokes access and removes this connector's token file. Existing ERP exports, logs and backups are subject to SMACKIN's separately confirmed retention process and are not deleted by disconnecting.

Questions and requests

Employees and authorized service providers should contact SMACKIN's ERP administrator or company management through established internal channels about access, corrections, disconnection or deletion. Company accounting records may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal purposes.
