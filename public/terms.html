Prepared November 11, 2026. Proposed terms for an internal application operated by Smackin', Inc.

Authorized internal use

SMACKIN ERP is for employees and service providers specifically authorized by Smackin', Inc. to support its accounting, reporting and operations. Access is not offered to the public. Users may connect only company records they have authority to access.

QuickBooks connection

A QuickBooks company administrator must approve the connection through Intuit. This connector is designed to retrieve accounting records and reports. It does not expose payment initiation or accounting write operations. Intuit controls the permissions available in its authorization screen.

Responsibilities

Protect access credentials, use company data only for assigned business duties, and report suspected unauthorized access to SMACKIN management. Do not share connection credentials or export company information to unapproved recipients.

Reporting and decisions

Reports depend on source-system completeness, reporting dates and accounting choices. Confirm material accounting or payment decisions against the underlying QuickBooks records and the company's approval procedures.

Disconnection and access removal

An authorized administrator may disconnect this connector or revoke it in QuickBooks. Revocation stops further access under that authorization. It does not erase QuickBooks records or previously exported reports. SMACKIN may remove internal access when it is no longer required.

Contact and changes

Contact SMACKIN's ERP administrator or company management through established internal channels. These internal application terms may be updated by Smackin', Inc.; any effective version will identify its effective date.
